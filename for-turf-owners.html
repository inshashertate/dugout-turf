What it does on a call

Takes the booking

Name, sport, day and time, then the customer's WhatsApp number — and it says nothing about the slot until it has checked your diary for real.

Never promises a slot it does not have

It checks by writing to your diary. The customer hears a price and a ground only once the slot is actually saved — never “it's free” followed by “sorry, it's gone”.

Knows both your grounds

With two grounds it gives whichever is free — cricket on one, football on the other in the same hour — and only says no when both are genuinely taken.

Quotes your prices

Weekday and weekend rates, hours multiplied correctly, advance and cancellation terms as you set them.

Applies your rules

Opening hours, minimum notice before a slot, whole-hour bookings — it will refuse politely and offer the earliest time it can actually give.

Handles cancellations

Finds the booking by phone number, reads it back, and cancels only after the customer clearly says yes. The hour goes back on sale immediately.

Speaks their language

English, Hindi, Hinglish or Marathi — whichever the caller starts in, for the whole call.

Tells you instantly

Every confirmed booking reaches you on WhatsApp or Telegram the moment it is made, with the name, number, slot, ground and amount.

What turf owners ask

What if two people call at the same time?

Both get answered. The agent takes as many calls at once as come in, and the diary decides who gets the slot — the second caller is told honestly and offered another time.

Will it understand Hindi or Marathi?

Yes. It replies in whatever the caller starts with — English, Hindi, Hinglish or Marathi — for the whole call.

Can it give away a slot I have already blocked?

No. Nothing is confirmed until it is written into the diary, and the diary refuses a slot that is already taken on both grounds.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Keep your number and point it at the agent, or start with the call button on your page and Instagram.

What about cancellations and advance bookings?

It finds the booking from the caller's number, reads it back, and cancels only when they clearly agree — the hour goes straight back on sale. Advance bookings for next week work the same as tonight.
