What it does on a call

Takes the booking

Name, sport, day and time, then the customer's WhatsApp number — asked only after they agree to the slot and the price.

Never promises a slot it does not have

The booking is written to your diary first. The customer hears “confirmed” only when the slot is actually saved, so two people can never hold the same hour.

Knows both your grounds

With two grounds it gives whichever is free — cricket on one, football on the other in the same hour — and only says no when both are genuinely taken.

Quotes your prices

Weekday and weekend rates, hours multiplied correctly, advance and cancellation terms as you set them.

Applies your rules

Opening hours, minimum notice before a slot, whole-hour bookings — it will refuse politely and offer the earliest time it can actually give.

Handles cancellations

Finds the booking by phone number, reads it back, and cancels only after the customer clearly says yes. The hour goes back on sale immediately.

Speaks their language

English, Hindi, Hinglish or Marathi — whichever the caller starts in, for the whole call.

Tells you instantly

Every confirmed booking reaches you on WhatsApp or Telegram the moment it is made, with the name, number, slot, ground and amount.
