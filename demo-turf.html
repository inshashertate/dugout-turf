How to book

  1. Tap the green “Book Greenfield Turf by Voice” button at the bottom-left and allow the microphone.
  2. Tell us your name, then whether you want to play cricket or football.
  3. Say the day, time and how many hours. Speak in English, Hindi, Hinglish or Marathi.
  4. Confirm the summary. Your payment link and confirmation come on WhatsApp.
